# SuperIntelligent Cat - credits

A GZDoom mod (Freedoom 2). Code, scripts and the generated art are original to this mod.

## 3D models (rendered into the Doom sprites BZOM, SHDW, HISS, BCAT and the bow arm)
- Kenney "Blocky Characters" 2.0 (character-l, character-e, character-n) - https://kenney.nl/assets/blocky-characters - Kenney (www.kenney.nl) - CC0 1.0
- Kenney "Cube Pets" (animal-cat, recoloured for the Hisser; with added glasses and cap for the SuperIntelligent Cat) - https://kenney.nl/assets/cube-pets - Kenney (www.kenney.nl) - CC0 1.0

## Generated with fal (AI image generation, made for this mod)
- Night sky with aurora and snowy peaks (CSKY*), strawberry (SBRY), winged strawberry frames (SBRW), dash crystal (DCRY), crystal heart (CHRT), spring (SPRG) - generated with fal (nano-banana-2); squash/stretch, used-crystal, golden strawberry and pain/death tints were derived from them by editing pixels.

## Block textures (mod/textures, MC*.png)
All textures below are derived from Minetest Game (https://github.com/minetest/minetest_game), commit snapshot of branch master downloaded 2026-10-04.
Changes: 16x16 sources upscaled x4 (nearest neighbour), overlays composited as Minetest does, alpha removed or thresholded to binary; doors cropped to the front face of the UV map and window holes filled. Licenses and authors are as stated in each mod's README.md / license.txt.
